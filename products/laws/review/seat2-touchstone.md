## What conforms

- **Domain Verdict and Touchstone verdict are separate types** (`Color::Verdict` vs `Color::Receipt`, `Op::Classify` vs `Op::Receipt`), and the map between them is a stated Formal law (`S.receipt.map`) with Bend, Haskell and Rust checks under the same id. The 4-value vocabulary HOLDS/BROKEN/BLOCKED/HOLE matches section 04 exactly.
- **F5**: `to_receipt(_, agree=false) = HOLE` for every verdict (law `receipt_map_hole`, proved by `{==}`); nothing is averaged. **F7**: `join_cover_missing_is_hole` by induction; a child receipt can never raise a system HOLDS.
- **Tier ladder honesty in the ontology**: `composed_laws_do_not_overclaim_their_tier` (weakest-link valuation) and `tiers_are_honest_about_ownership` (core-owned stages never claimed Formal on the products side) both run and pass; the builder reports they caught 3 overclaims.
- **Operadic structure**: typed wiring, every slot fed once, depth ≤ 3, every global law derived, acyclic derivations, no derivation through a Hole — all executed as tests (12/12).
- **Gate path has no Python**: `atom_gate.sh` is bash + bend + runghc + cargo only. `cargo-mutants` receipts exist for layer 2 (12 caught, 1 missed = `main` of the printer, 6 unviable).
- **G0 core invariance** is mechanical (`sha256sum -c`), and no file under `crates/` changed on the branch.
- **Bend proofs are not trusted alone**: the Haskell correlate and the Rust transcription re-check every Bend law id (the brief's two-oracle rule for the unaudited compiler), and `bend --verdict` (Lean-checked kernel) is a separate gate step.
- The builder's own `review/meta-operad.md` labels itself "NOT the independent check" and surfaces MO-01 (Extend's output unconsumed) and MO-03 (no budget) honestly.
